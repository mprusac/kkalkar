# KK Alkar Sinj

We are looking to create a modern, responsive website for the Bosnian basketball club KK Posušje, inspired heavily by the recent redesign of the HKK Mostar website (https://www.hkkmostar.ba/).
We have attached screenshots from HKK Mostar’s site for visual reference, and you will also receive KK Posušje’s team photo and logo to work with.

⚫🟡 Project Scope & Style

The visual identity should follow a yellow and black theme, matching KK Posušje’s club colors.

Maintain the same modern design feel and animations as HKK Mostar’s site (e.g. smooth loading, animated numbers, bold typography).

Font can be the same or similar to the one used on HKK Mostar’s site.

All major structural sections from HKK Mostar should be included, with a few improvements outlined below.

📄 Page Structure & Content

The website should include the following main navigation sections (in this order):

Početna (Home) – Hero image with players, club name, slogan, and a CTA button (e.g. “Pridruži se”)

Rezultati (Results) – Section with the most recent match results; each result should link externally to the corresponding SofaScore statistics page.

Vijesti (News) – Same structure as HKK Mostar, with cards for match reports, transfers, and announcements.

Tim (Team) – Profile cards with player photos, names, positions, and key stats.

Galerija (Gallery) – Match photo albums, like the Mostar site (grid view).

O Klubu (About) – Short history, mission, youth categories, and club stats (animated counters).

Kontakt (Contact) – New section (not present on HKK Mostar): includes a contact form (submits to club’s email), club email, phone (optional), and an embedded Google Map location.

🛠️ Functional Features

Full admin panel (CMS) for club staff to upload news posts and images (just like HKK Mostar).

Include contact form functionality (to email address that we’ll provide).

Responsive layout (mobile/tablet friendly).

Loading animations for numbers (e.g., number of members, categories, years).

Sponsor section (use same layout and placeholder logos for now as in HKK Mostar).

📝 About Us (for website section)

KK Posušje is a proud basketball club from the heart of Western Herzegovina, with a tradition spanning over two decades. The club gathers boys and young men from across the region, developing them from youth levels to the senior team competing in Bosnia and Herzegovina’s top basketball league. We are committed to nurturing young talent, fostering community spirit, and promoting sportsmanship.

🏆 Achievements

2021: Promoted to the Bosnian Premier League after winning the First League

Multiple youth championships in the Herceg-Bosna region

Victories and participation in tournaments across Bosnia and Croatia

Several youth players selected for national U16 and U18 teams

Vision

To build a strong base of young athletes and promote basketball as a positive foundation for the community. KK Posušje aims to become a recognized club in the region through youth development, sports values, and professionalism – with continued investment in infrastructure and coaching quality.
